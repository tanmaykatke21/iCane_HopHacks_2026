# iCane

A phone-camera walk-assist cane that gives visually impaired users real-time situational awareness while navigating their environment.

## How it works

- A phone is attached to the cane using a simple clamp
- The camera continuously captures the surrounding scene
- Computer vision and scene understanding analyze nearby objects and hazards
- The system alerts the user about relevant risks in real time
- The user can also query the environment to understand what is around them

## Functionality

iCane turns a standard cane into a smart navigation aid using an iPhone camera mounted to the cane. The app continuously monitors the environment and identifies hazards in real time.

Users can:

- detect static objects in their path
- identify people approaching from the side
- spot stairs and curbs
- recognize low overhangs
- locate doors and obstacles nearby
- understand cluttered floor conditions
- query the scene with a tap for instant feedback
